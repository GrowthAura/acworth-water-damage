Is water still coming in?

While you wait for the crew

  • Shut off the water at the main valve or appliance.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms if you can reach the breaker from a dry spot.
  • Move people and pets away from sagging ceilings and standing water.
  • Photograph and video every wet area.
  • Move valuables and electronics to a dry room.
  • Stay out of sewage or floodwater.
