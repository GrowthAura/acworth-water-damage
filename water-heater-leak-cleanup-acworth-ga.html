What happens when a water heater fails in an Acworth basement or garage?

A failed tank releases its full volume plus incoming supply water until shut off, soaking basement floors, walls and stored belongings.

A typical home water heater holds 40 to 80 gallons. When the tank itself splits, that water comes out at once, and the cold water supply keeps feeding it until someone closes the valve. In a finished basement, that means carpet, drywall and anything stored on the floor. In a garage, water spreads under the door into the house. In an upstairs closet or attic, it comes through the ceiling. A water heater leak in Acworth needs both a fast shut-off and a fast cleanup.

How to shut off a leaking water heater

  • Turn off the power or gas: flip the breaker for an electric heater or turn the gas valve to "off" or "pilot."
  • Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe going into the top of the tank.
  • If that valve won't turn, shut off the main water to the house.
  • Keep away from hot water, which can scald.
  • Move belongings out of the water's path.
  • Call for water removal so drying starts quickly.
  • Only a plumber should drain, repair or replace the tank.

Tank failure is the one that floods a room. Rust inside the tank eats through the steel over years, and once it gives out, it doesn't stop on its own.

Drain pan overflow and slow leaks

Many water heaters sit in a drain pan meant to catch small leaks and carry water to a drain. Drain pan overflow happens when the pan has no drain line, the line is clogged or the leak is bigger than the pan can handle. Slow leaks are sneaky: water seeps under flooring and into the bottom of walls for weeks before anyone notices. Signs include rust at the base of the tank, damp flooring nearby, swollen baseboards and a musty smell in the utility area.

Garage water heater leaks

A garage water heater leak looks less serious because the floor is concrete, but water often runs under the door into the house, soaking the bottom of the wall shared with the living space, the laundry room or a hallway. Stored boxes, tools and drywall on the garage walls soak up water too. Garage water heater cleanup includes checking the shared wall and any rooms on the other side.

Some Acworth homes have water heaters in hall closets or attics. These leaks are the most damaging because water travels through the floor into ceilings and walls below. Attic and upstairs closet units should always sit in a drain pan with a working drain line. See ceiling water damage repair in Acworth

How long do water heaters last?

Tank water heaters typically last about 8 to 12 years. Many Acworth homes built in the 1990s and early 2000s have tanks that are at or past that age. Signs a tank is near the end:

  • Rusty or discolored hot water
  • Rumbling or popping noises from sediment buildup
  • Rust around the base or fittings
  • Moisture under the tank
  • Hot water running out faster than it used to
  • The age is usually printed in the serial number on the tank label.

How much does leaking water heater cleanup cost in Acworth?

Leaking water heater cleanup cost depends on where the tank is and how far the water spread. A garage leak caught quickly stays at the lower end. A tank that failed in a finished basement or attic, soaking carpet, drywall or ceilings, often falls in the typical $1,400 to $3,800 range for mitigation, with repairs on top. Replacing the water heater itself is a separate plumbing cost.

Is water heater damage covered by insurance?

Most Georgia homeowners policies cover sudden water damage from a burst water heater, like soaked floors and walls. The water heater itself usually isn't covered, since it failed from age or wear. Slow leaks that went on for a long time may be excluded as gradual damage. See water damage insurance claim help in Acworth

Water Heater Leak FAQs

Can I use hot water after a leak?

+

Not until a plumber inspects or replaces the tank. Running it with a leak can damage the heater and spread more water.

How long does it take to dry after a water heater leak?

+

Usually 3 to 5 days, longer if water reached a basement slab or block walls.

Do I need to replace drywall?

+

Only if it wicked water high, stayed wet too long or is crumbling. Firm drywall wet with clean water is often dried in place.

Is water heater water clean?

+

It starts as clean water, but it picks up rust and sediment, and it can become contaminated if it sits on carpet or reaches a floor drain.

Who replaces the water heater?

+

A plumber replaces the tank. Our crew handles the water removal, drying and repairs.
