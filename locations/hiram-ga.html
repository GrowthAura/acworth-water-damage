What should Hiram homeowners do first when a pipe bursts in winter?

Shut off the main valve, cut power to wet rooms, photograph the damage and get extraction started before water spreads under floors and into walls.

When a burst pipe in Hiram, GA starts spraying, every minute adds gallons to your floors and walls. Most pipe bursts happen in attics, crawl spaces, garages and exterior walls, where pipes are closest to cold air. The main shut-off is usually where the water line enters the house, near the water heater, in the garage or in a crawl space access. Knowing where yours is before you need it can save thousands in damage.
