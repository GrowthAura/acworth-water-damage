Flooded basement in Kennesaw: why it happens so often

A flooded basement in Kennesaw usually comes down to slope, soil and water management. Homes built into hillsides have buried walls on one side, and Georgia clay holds water against them after storms. When gutters dump next to the foundation or the sump pump can't keep up, water finds the joint where the wall meets the slab. In finished basements, carpet, pad and lower drywall soak it up quickly, and basement flooding can turn into mold within two days if drying doesn't start. Fast pumping, extraction and dehumidification save most of the structure.
