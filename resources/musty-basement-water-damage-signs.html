You finished the basement, the AC is running and it still smells musty. That's one of the most common complaints from Acworth homeowners, and it almost always means moisture is getting in somewhere. Here are seven signs that point to hidden water damage, why basements around Acworth are so prone to it, and what to do before a smell turns into a mold problem.

What causes a musty smell in a basement?

A musty basement smell comes from mold or mildew growing on damp materials, usually because water is seeping in or humidity is staying too high.

That earthy odor is made by gases mold gives off as it grows. Mold needs moisture, and basements get it from three places: water coming through the walls or floor, leaks from plumbing and appliances, and humid air that condenses on cool surfaces. In Acworth, where many homes are built into clay hillsides and summers are humid, all three show up often.

The 7 hidden signs of water damage in a basement

1. The smell gets stronger after rain

If the odor spikes a day or two after a storm, groundwater is likely getting in through the walls or floor.

2. White, chalky residue on block walls

That powder is efflorescence: minerals left behind when water moves through concrete or block and evaporates. It's proof water is passing through the wall.

3. Swollen or separating baseboards

Baseboards along exterior walls that swell, crack or pull away often mean the bottom of the drywall behind them is wet.

4. Damp carpet near the walls

Carpet that feels cool or damp along the edges, or a pad that's darker than the rest, is a classic sign of seepage where the wall meets the slab.

5. Bubbling paint or stains low on walls

Paint bubbles and brown or yellow lines near the floor show moisture wicking up from below.

6. Rust on metal near the floor

Rust on water heater bases, furnace stands, tack strips or nails points to ongoing moisture.

7. Condensation and sticky air

Water beading on pipes or windows, or air that feels heavy, means humidity is too high, and the basement's cool surfaces are collecting moisture.

Will a dehumidifier fix it?

A dehumidifier helps when the problem is humid air, and it's worth running in any Acworth basement during summer. It won't fix water coming through walls or a hidden leak, and it can't dry out a soaked pad or wet drywall. If the smell comes back after rain or the meter shows wet walls, the moisture source needs to be found and the wet materials dried or replaced.

FAQs

Is a musty basement smell dangerous?

+

The smell itself means mold or mildew is growing, which can cause allergy-type symptoms in sensitive people. The source should be found and fixed.

Can I just paint over stains?

+

Not until the moisture is fixed and the wall is dry. Painting over a damp wall hides the problem and traps moisture.

Why does my finished basement smell even with the AC on?

+

The AC cools the air but may not remove enough moisture from a basement, and seepage behind walls or under carpet keeps feeding the smell.
