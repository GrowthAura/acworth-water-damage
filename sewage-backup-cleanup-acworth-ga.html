Who is responsible when sewage backs up into an Acworth home: you or Cobb County?

Cobb County Water System maintains the public sewer mains; the homeowner owns the private sewer line on their property. Report suspected main problems to Cobb Water Emergency Dispatch.

The pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the street, called the sewer lateral, belongs to you up to where it connects with the county main. If the blockage is in that private line, the repair is yours. If the county main backs up, Cobb County Water System handles the main, and the county can check whether its lines, including any that run through an easement on your property, are part of the problem. Call Emergency Dispatch at 770-419-6201 if you see sewage in your yard or basement and suspect the main. For damage inside the home, the county generally points homeowners to their own insurance first.

Why sewage cleanup in Acworth, GA needs a professional crew

Sewage is Category 3 black water, the most contaminated class under the IICRC S500 standard. It can carry bacteria, viruses and parasites that cause serious illness. That changes how the cleanup is done:

  • Protective suits, gloves and respirators are required
  • Contaminated porous materials are removed, not dried
  • Every surface is cleaned and disinfected before drying starts
  • Air scrubbers capture airborne particles during removal
  • Children, pets and anyone with health issues stay away until the area is cleared
  • Category 3 black water spreads fast into drywall, carpet and wood, so every hour matters.

What should you do right away after a sewer backup?

  • Stop using water: no flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher, since all of it adds to the backup.
  • Keep everyone out of the affected area, especially kids and pets.
  • Turn off power to the area only if you can reach the breaker safely from a dry spot.
  • Don't touch sewage with bare skin; wear boots and gloves if you have to go in.
  • Don't use household cleaners or fans on sewage; they spread contamination.
  • Photograph everything for insurance.
  • Call for sewage cleanup so removal and disinfection start fast.

Sewer backup in the basement: the most common Acworth call

Basements are the lowest drain point in the house, so a sewer backup in the basement in Acworth usually shows up at the floor drain, the basement toilet or the shower first. In finished basements that means carpet, drywall and built-ins all come into contact with sewage at once. Because these spaces are also humid, quick drying after disinfection is key to keeping mold from following. See flooded basement cleanup in Acworth

What causes sewage backups in Acworth homes?

Grease
cooking grease poured down drains hardens in pipes. Grease is one of the most common causes of sewer overflows Cobb County crews clear, including on Acworth streets.
Wipes and paper products
"flushable" wipes don't break down like toilet paper.

Heavy rain

Septic backup in 30102 and the Cherokee County side

Some homes in Acworth's 30102 ZIP code, especially in unincorporated Cherokee County and older lake-area properties, use septic systems instead of county sewer. A septic backup usually happens when the tank is full, the drain field is saturated after heavy rain or a line between the house and tank is blocked. The cleanup inside the home is the same Category 3 process. The tank and drain field are handled by a septic service.

Signs of a septic backup:

  • Slow drains throughout the house
  • Gurgling toilets or drains
  • Sewage odor indoors or near the drain field
  • Water backing up in the lowest drain or tub

Does insurance cover sewage backup cleanup in Acworth?

Many standard homeowners policies exclude sewer and drain backup unless you've added a water backup endorsement. Check your policy for that coverage. If the backup came from a county main, Cobb County typically asks homeowners to file with their own insurance first. Keep photos, a list of removed items and the cleanup documentation for any claim. Sewage Backup Cleanup FAQs

Is sewage backup dangerous?

+

Yes. Sewage is Category 3 black water and can carry bacteria and viruses. Avoid contact and keep children and pets away.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

+

Very small toilet overflows on tile can be cleaned with protective gear and disinfectant. Anything that reached carpet, drywall or a basement should be handled professionally.

How long does sewage cleanup take?

+

Removal and disinfection usually take a day or two, followed by about 3 to 5 days of drying before rebuilding.

Will the smell go away?

+

Yes, once contaminated materials are removed, surfaces are disinfected and the area is dried. Lingering odor is treated with air scrubbers and deodorization.

+
